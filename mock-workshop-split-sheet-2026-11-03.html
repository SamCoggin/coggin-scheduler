The Overbury job as it would print. One card, one plan, one due date. The sheet splits into a page per workshop by the product category on each line: chairs, stools, sofas, soft seating, booths and pods are seating, everything else (desks, bench desks, screens, storage, tables) is furniture. Each page carries only its own lines and its own finished count, with the whole job's count beside it. A job that is all chairs prints the seating page only.
