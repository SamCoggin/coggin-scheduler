E · the rules

A production job goes to seating or furniture from its items; a mixed job is in both with its own count in each. Estimates come from the standard per item, as the Manager view already works them out, and the queue is in due order. Capacity to a due date is the owner's working days, plus a cover's spare, plus anyone pulled in for named days. The owner's day flows down the queue, so nobody is ever planned twice. The support pool is on recycling, deliveries and clearances unless pulled in, and the tally and the rounds already know that. The per-person grid stays for site work and for pinning someone to a job on a day when you want to; it stops being the way production is planned. Changing who owns a workshop is one setting, so the hand-over from Bart to Jordan is a switch, not a rebuild. Later, when Jordan starts showing Jack furniture, the cover works both ways and the skill matrix is the two owner settings plus who covers what.
